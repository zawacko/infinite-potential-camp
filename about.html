Why Infinite Potential?

We're five middle schoolers who love math so much we decided to share it. Campers are sorted into small groups by skill level after a friendly placement test, so everyone learns at just the right pace — and has a blast doing it.

🎯

Just-Right Groups

A quick placement test on day one sorts campers into small groups so the math is never too easy or too hard.

🤝

Run by Real Kids

Five passionate middle schoolers lead the fun — close enough in age to make math feel friendly and exciting.

🎲

Games & Puzzles

Brain teasers, math games, and hands-on challenges keep every day playful and full of "aha!" moments.

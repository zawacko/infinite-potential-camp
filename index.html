Why Infinite Potential?

We're five middle schoolers who love math so much we decided to share it. Campers are sorted into small groups by skill level after a friendly placement test, so everyone learns at just the right pace — and has a blast doing it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question? We've got answers! Tap any question to learn more.

Who is the camp for?

Infinite Potential is for rising 2nd through 6th graders who love math — or are just getting excited about it! Odd weeks are for rising grades 4–6 and even weeks are for rising grades 2–3.

How are campers grouped?

On the first morning, everyone takes a friendly placement test. We then sort campers into small groups by skill level so the math is never too easy or too hard — and everyone learns at just the right pace.

Who runs the camp?

Five passionate middle schoolers who love math, plus a helper. We're close enough in age to make math feel friendly, fun, and exciting. Meet us in the Team section!

What are the camp hours?

Full-day campers attend 9:00 AM – 3:30 PM. Half-day campers join us in the morning through lunch. Check out the full daily schedule for details.

Are snacks and lunch provided?

Snacks are included every day! Lunch is optional — you can pack your own, or add daily lunch to a full-day plan for just $45/week.

What should my child bring?

Pencils & pens, an eraser, a ruler, and a water bottle. A computer or tablet is strongly recommended (but optional). See the full What to Bring list.

How much does it cost?

Full Day is $225/week and Half Day is $150/week (5 days). Sign up for one week or all summer long — you can mix and match weeks! See Pricing for details and discounts.

Do you offer any discounts?

Yes! Our family & friends get 25% off plus free lunch with a family discount code. Enter your code on the registration form and it's applied automatically.

How do I sign up?

Right now you can register for our free trial camp on the registration form below — just fill in your details and submit. Summer 2027 registration opens soon!

Why Infinite Potential?

We're five middle schoolers who love math so much we decided to share it. Campers are sorted into small groups by skill level after a friendly placement test, so everyone learns at just the right pace — and has a blast doing it.

🎯

Just-Right Groups

A quick placement test on day one sorts campers into small groups so the math is never too easy or too hard.

🤝

Run by Real Kids

Five passionate middle schoolers lead the fun — close enough in age to make math feel friendly and exciting.

🎲

Games & Puzzles

Brain teasers, math games, and hands-on challenges keep every day playful and full of "aha!" moments.

What to Bring

Pack these in a backpack each day and you'll be ready for math adventures!

✏️

Pencils & Pens

Bring a few so you never run out mid-problem.

🧽

An Eraser

Mistakes are part of learning — erase and try again!

📏

A Ruler

Perfect for measuring, drawing, and geometry fun.

📝

Scratch Paper Optional

Handy for working out problems — but we'll have some on hand too!

💻

A Computer or Tablet Strongly Recommended

For interactive activities and online math games — strongly recommended, but optional if you can't bring one.

💧

A Water Bottle

Stay hydrated and refreshed all day long.

🍎

Snacks & Lunch Optional

Free snacks are provided at camp! Pack your own lunch, or add daily lunch to your plan for an extra cost.

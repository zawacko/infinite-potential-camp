What to Bring

Pack these in a backpack each day and you'll be ready for math adventures!

  • Pencils & pens — bring a few so you never run out mid-problem.
  • An eraser — mistakes are part of learning, so erase and try again!
  • A ruler — perfect for measuring, drawing, and geometry fun.
  • A water bottle — stay hydrated and refreshed all day long.
  • A computer or tablet Strongly Recommended — for interactive activities and online math games (but optional if you can't bring one).
  • Scratch paper Optional — handy for working out problems, but we'll have some on hand too.
  • Snacks & lunch Optional — free snacks are provided at camp! Pack your own lunch, or add daily lunch to your plan for an extra cost.
